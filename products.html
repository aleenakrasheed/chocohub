Product Information

Important details to check before enjoying or gifting our chocolates.

🍫 Ingredients

Depending on the product, ingredients may include cocoa, cocoa butter, sugar, milk solids, and permitted flavourings or inclusions. Check the actual product label for its complete ingredient list.

⚠️ Allergen Warning

Some varieties may contain milk, soy, nuts, or other allergens. Cross-contact may also be possible. Confirm the specific ingredients and allergen declaration for each batch before publishing.

🌡️ Storage Instructions

Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and strong odours. Follow the temperature and storage directions printed on the actual product packaging.

📅 Shelf Life

Shelf life varies by recipe, fillings, packaging, and storage conditions. Refer to the verified best-before date on each product label.

🏷️ Manufacturing Details

Before sale, display the actual manufacturer's name and address, batch or lot number, manufacturing or packing date as applicable, and required food-label details.

📦 Best Before

Check the date printed on the package before consuming. Do not use a generic date on this website as a substitute for the actual product label.
